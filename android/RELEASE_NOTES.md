**To install on an Android phone or tablet:** open this page on the phone, tap **PhosphorLoop.apk** below, then tap **Install** (Android may first ask you to allow installs from your browser, and Google Play Protect may ask you to confirm, since the app doesn't come from the Play Store).

Already installed? Install this one the same way. It replaces the old version and keeps your presets.

Needs Android 10 or newer.
