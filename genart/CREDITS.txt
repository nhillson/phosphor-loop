Moving generative art for Phosphor Loop's Generative art input.

Every piece here was drawn by the genart command-line tool (cmd/genart) from
jdxyw/generativeart, https://github.com/jdxyw/generativeart
Copyright (c) 2021 jdxyw, MIT License.

Each is 1024 x 1024, one full cycle of the generator's animation (240 frames), so it loops without a seam; for the
three whose cycle doesn't quite close (Droste spiral, Kleinian circles, Bismuth crystal) the last 2 seconds are blended into
the first (180 frames).
The frames were made with the command below, then put together at 30 frames a second (8 seconds) as VP9 WebM:
  ffmpeg -i x.apng -vf "setpts=N/30/TB,format=yuv420p" -r 30 -c:v libvpx-vp9 -crf 42 -b:v 0 x.webm
