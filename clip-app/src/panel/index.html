      Add the button to Chrome

      Once, on this computer. About a minute. Chrome needs you to do these steps yourself.

      1. Open Chrome’s extensions page. Press Copy the address, click Chrome’s address bar, paste (Ctrl+V) and press Enter.
      2. Turn on Developer mode, the switch at the top right of that page.
      3. Press Load unpacked (top left). In the window that opens, click the address bar at its top, paste (Ctrl+V), press Enter, then press Select Folder.
      4. Pin the button so it’s always there: click the puzzle piece (a) at the top right of Chrome, then the pin (b) beside Send to Phosphor Clips.
      Waiting for the button to appear in Chrome…

      Then, on any page with a video: press the button for Start here / End here and Grab, or right-click a video, GIF or link. Phosphor Clips must be running (it can sit by the clock); if it isn’t, the button offers to start it.
